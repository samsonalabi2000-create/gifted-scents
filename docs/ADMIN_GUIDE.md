# Store owner guide

Sign in at **/login** with your admin account. You land on the **Overview**.

## Add a perfume (or roll-on, candle, soap…)
1. **Products > Add product**
2. Pick the **Product type** (Perfume, Roll-On, Body Mist, Oil Perfume, Candle, Air Freshener, Pocket-Sized Scent, Diffuser, Body Cream, Soap, Scrub, Gift set…). Fragrance notes only appear for types that need them
3. Name, brand, category, who it is for
4. **Sizes, prices & stock**: one row per size (e.g. 50ml, 100ml, Decant). Each has its own SKU, price, optional sale price and stock
5. **Photos**: drag them in; the first one is the main photo; use the arrows to reorder; add alt text
6. Fragrance details (optional): family, notes, longevity, projection, occasions
7. Tick Featured / Bestseller / New arrival if wanted
8. **Save & publish**. Unpublish any time without deleting

## Handle an order
**Orders > open the order.** Paid card orders arrive as *Paid* automatically. For a bank transfer, press **Confirm payment received** after checking your account. Then move it: Processing → Ready for dispatch → Shipped (add rider/tracking details) → Delivered. Cancelling returns stock. Everything is logged.
An order with a red warning was paid but stock ran out: contact the customer or refund.

## Support
**Support > open a request > Reply.** Customers get your reply by email. Use *Internal notes* for team-only comments and set the status (New, Open, In progress, Resolved, Closed).

## Reviews
**Reviews:** Approve, Reject, Hide or Delete. Tap **Show on homepage** to put a review in the *Orbit of Notes* section.

## Stock
Edit stock in the product editor, or watch the **Low stock** list on the Overview. The warning level is in Settings > Store.

## Change the website without a developer
**Settings:** store details, logo, WhatsApp number, social links (only filled ones show), announcement bar, homepage headline/button text/image, FAQ, policies, delivery zones and fees, coupons, assistant name/greeting.
